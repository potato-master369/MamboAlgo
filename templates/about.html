What is MamboAlgo?

MamboAlgo is a simple algorithm to sort an image into Matikanetannhauser (yes). It works with almost anything that has brightness differences. Trust me bro.


Is this free?

Hell yeah! You can get a copy from GitHub and run it locally, with no internet required, and have all the fun of mambofying your holiday photos on the plane :D
